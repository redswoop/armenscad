# Handoff: viewer performance and flicker (2026-10-28, overnight)

Armen: "things feel a bit slow at times, esp. the bones arm with the presence meshes. Lots of flicker as I
orbit. On the 1x display." Measured in a DevTools page at his window size (2245 x 1161 CSS px, device ratio 1),
bones model (4 bones 94 k triangles + 4 LDraw reference parts 1.38 M triangles), a scripted orbit, 2 s per
configuration. Machine: this Mac; his Brave window on the external monitor is the same GPU.

## What it was

| configuration (before)                     | avg ms | p95 ms | draw calls | triangles / frame |
|--------------------------------------------|-------:|-------:|-----------:|------------------:|
| section on, shadows, edges, pixel ratio 2  |   23.3 |   34.0 |         92 |         4,436,102 |
| same at pixel ratio 1                      |   16.7 |   18.2 |         92 |         4,436,102 |
| section off                                |   16.7 |   18.1 |         18 |         1,474,850 |
| LDraw parts hidden                         |   16.7 |   18.2 |         68 |           294,808 |

16.7 ms is the 60 Hz cap, so everything under it is "fine on this GPU"; what shows is what pushed over it.
- The pixel ratio 2 I added earlier that day (so 1x displays get antialiased hairlines) was the one thing that
  broke 60 fps on its own: 4x the fill, 43 fps with stutters to 30.
- The section drew every part three times (two stencil passes + colour) whether or not the plane touched it,
  and the shadow map re-rendered every part every frame although the key light never moves.
- EdgesGeometry on the three big LDraw parts takes 0.5-0.8 s each on the main thread (once per load/rebuild).
- The flicker: each LDraw reference part shares its whole bounding box with its bone and 23-38 % of its
  vertices sit on the bone's surface (same offset, same depth), so the depth test winner changes pixel by pixel
  as you orbit: grey speckle crawling over every coloured face. Screenshot-verified before/after.

## Done (all uncommitted, with the gizmo/badge work from the same day)

- `scene.js`: pixel ratio 2 only when the camera is still, the display's own ratio while it moves (switches
  0.2 s after the last `camera` event). Moving frames hold 60 fps; still frames are supersampled.
- `shading.js`: `shadowMap.autoUpdate = false`; `dirtyShadows()` on parts / visibility events, shadow refit,
  shading changes; `section.js` marks it when the plane or the on/off changes (clipShadows depends on it).
  If a shadow ever looks stale, something changed a caster without one of those; add a `dirtyShadows()` there.
- `section.js` syncCaps: stencil passes and caps only for parts whose world bounding box the plane crosses
  (exact: a part wholly on the kept side nets a count of 0, one wholly on the cut side is clipped away).
- Manifest `"reference": true` per part (server.js passes it; SKILL.md documents it): parts.js draws it with
  polygonOffset 3/3 (behind the design where faces coincide), shading.js gives it no edge lines (WebGL cannot
  offset lines, so the reference's creases drew through the design's faces; also saves the EdgesGeometry
  build). The bones manifest flags its four ldraw parts (uncommitted in the bones repo).

After: section on, shadows, edges, refs flagged: 16.7 avg / 18.0 p95, 64 calls, 2.48 M triangles (the plane at
x=0 crosses one bone and its reference); section off 1.47 M. Speckle gone on every face.

## Not done / ideas, in order of payoff

- The LDraw presence meshes are 1.38 M triangles for four bricks: decimate them; nothing else comes close.
  (Armen asked: "decimate" is mesh-speak for edge-collapse simplification to any target count, not /10.) Target
  ~50 k each (7x) is indistinguishable at viewer scale; ~10 k still reads as the brick, and they are only a
  position reference. Route that fits: a pipeline step (python, `trimesh` + `pyfqmr` or `open3d`) run once per
  part the manifest marks `reference`, cached beside the source STL; a one-off in MeshLab / Blender Decimate
  also works. OpenSCAD has no decimator. three.js `SimplifyModifier` at load time is too slow at this size.
  Alternative: load them as a `reference` STL ghost instead of parts.
- EdgesGeometry off the main thread (a worker) or cached per part version on the server.
- Shadow map 2048 PCFSoft: 1024 is likely invisible at this scale; try when the map is dirty often (drags).
- The grid and axes are 1 px GL lines; at ratio 1 (moving) they alias. Fat lines (Line2) if it bothers him.
- Section drags re-render the shadow map every frame (the plane moves); fine so far.

## Incident, so it does not repeat

Three `armenscad browse` sessions were running, two started with PORT=3003 (roots `projects/` and
`projects/bionicle/`). Editing `server.js` restarted both `bun --watch` children and the `projects/` one won the
port, so the viewer at 5180 (root bionicle) served the wrong workspace ("connecting…", /bones/ 404). I killed the
duplicate `projects/`+3003 tree and nudged the bionicle one back onto 3003. The `projects/` session on 3004 is
untouched. Lesson: `lsof -iTCP:3003 -sTCP:LISTEN` and `/api/root` before and after any server.js edit.
